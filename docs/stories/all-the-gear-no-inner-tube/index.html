All the gear. No inner tube.

The expedition luggage contains a coffee maker. The puncture repair equipment is somebody else’s problem.

Anyone near the TET with a front inner tube, tyre levers, a pump and experience fitting them? Ideally someone who can come to me.

First proper off-road trip on the big bike. Front’s gone flat despite being in Enduro mode, which I’ll be raising with the dealer.

Before the usual comments, I have prepared. Full aluminium luggage, expedition screen, auxiliary lights and the premium navigation subscription. Didn’t bring tools because we were trying to travel light.

Location is difficult to explain. I’m on the green bit of the map. Can send a screenshot once someone tells me how to get the route back up.

Please no lectures about training. I’ve been driving for 23 years and watched most of a video about standing up.

There are two of us, but we agreed beforehand that neither would lift anything heavy because we’re on holiday.

EDIT: Sorted for coffee. Just need the tube, tools, pump and person.

EDIT 2: Someone on a small Honda stopped but immediately started talking about tyre pressures, so we’re waiting for an adventure rider.

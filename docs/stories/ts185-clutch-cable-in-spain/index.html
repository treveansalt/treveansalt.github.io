Anyone near Teruel with a clutch cable for a TS185?

A brilliant little Suzuki, a cable left on the bench at home, and a solo Spanish trip proceeding at sitting-down speed.

Bit of a long shot. I'm doing the Spanish TET on my old Suzuki TS185 and the clutch cable has snapped. Currently off the track in the shade, bike's fine otherwise.

It's a brilliant little thing. Light enough to pick up, lovely on these tracks and you don't need a laptop to work on it. It is a little old now, admittedly. Mine's a late 70s one, although the bloke I bought it from wasn't completely certain which year.

The lever had been getting heavier for a while. I put some oil down the cable before leaving and it felt better, so I thought that was sorted. It did the whole ferry journey without any trouble, which felt encouraging at the time.

I've walked back to a village and shown the cable to a garage. Really helpful chap, but he hasn't got anything that fits. Found one online, although the seller wants a photo of both ends and some measurements before he'll confirm it. Earliest delivery is next week.

I'm travelling on my own. Thought that would make things simpler as I could go at my own pace. Currently my own pace is sitting beside a Suzuki.

I've got tools, spare plugs, tubes and plenty of two-stroke oil. No spare cable. There was one on the bench at home, but I left it because I was trying to keep the luggage down.

Does anyone know a shop round here that could make one up, or happen to be heading this way with something suitable? Happy to pay, obviously.

Please don't turn this into a modern bike versus old bike thing. She's been absolutely faultless apart from this, the fuel tap and the thing on Tuesday.

Photo from this morning. Still think she's awesome.
